The Main Event

USDA Prime Ribeye. Wet-aged 21 to 28 days, cut thick, cooked sous vide to medium rare, seared, sliced and shared. Compound butter on the side.

Sides

Tallow Roasted Potatoes in Wagyu-cross beef tallow. Charred Broccoli with lemon and garlic. Calabrian Chili Cauliflower, Hilary's signature, with a kick.

Salads

Caesar with croutons and Parmesan on the side. Fennel and Citrus with arugula, orange, toasted nuts, and a sharp vinaigrette.

Dessert

Dark chocolate and fresh berries. Light, because you will have earned it.

After Dinner

Armagnac. Brandy's older, more interesting cousin from Gascony.

Dietary Intel

Almost everything is gluten-free and dairy-free by design. Butter and croutons live on the side so you can opt out.
